# Third-Party and Extra Plugins
These are plugins that are made by third parties, not common, actively maintained or are otherwise unsuitable for main distribution.

### Adding Your Plugin
If you wish to add your plugin to this list, please edit this page and add a new row in the "Third-Party Plugins" table.

Your plugin's wiki page must be located under `/plugins` on this site, for example, `/plugins/my_plugin_name`. `my_plugin_name` must correspond to your plugin's name as it is used in the config file.
